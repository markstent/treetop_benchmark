Canopy Hollow

A storybook village high in the giant trees. Orbit in close — planks, ropes, windows, and porch clutter are modeled, not painted on.

  • Orbit — left drag orbits, right drag pans, scroll zooms. The camera stays above the ground.
  • Walk (V) — click to capture the mouse, WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to hurry. Walk platforms, bridges, stairs, and the lift.
  • R resets the view. H toggles this card. Esc releases the mouse.
