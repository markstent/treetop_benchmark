Welcome to the treetops

A small village of rope bridges, lantern light and woodsmoke, high above the forest floor.

Orbit mode

  • Left drag Orbit around the village
  • Right drag Pan (or hold Shift)
  • Wheel Zoom toward the cursor
  • Pinch Zoom and pan on a trackpad

Walk mode

  • W A S D Walk on platforms and bridges
  • Shift Run
  • Mouse Look (click the view first)
  • Esc Release the mouse

Anywhere

  • F Toggle orbit / walk
  • R Reset the view
  • 1-4 Spring, summer, autumn, winter
  • H Show or hide this help
