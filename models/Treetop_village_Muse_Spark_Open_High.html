🌿 Welcome to the Canopy Village

A storybook treetop settlement woven between seven ancient trees. Every plank, rope and lantern is procedural — no external models.

  • Orbit: left-drag orbit, right-drag pan, wheel zoom. Damped & limited below ground.
  • Walk: press C or “Enter Walk Mode”, click canvas to lock pointer, WASD + Mouse look. Esc to exit lock.
  • Use the right panel to change time of day (watch lanterns & windows light up), season, fog, wind and rebuild the village.
  • From far away read the silhouette; up close inspect door frames, shingles, barrels, laundry & birdhouses.
